01 · Brand essence

Precise. Engaging. Effortless.

QED Webinars is the online and hybrid arm of QED, a Brussels event agency. Our audience is senior and time-poor: institutions, associations and executives. The brand must feel premium but never flashy, institutional but never boring.

Promise: Webinars that stand out — flawlessly produced, genuinely engaging, and handled end-to-end or à la carte.

Precise

Rehearsed, on time, no surprises. Our design is ordered and calm.

Engaging

We turn viewers into participants. Green marks the moments that matter.

Expert

Rooted in the EU quarter. We speak with quiet authority.

Flexible

Pick-and-choose services. Modular layouts that adapt to any format.

02 · Logo

The wordmark

The QED wordmark is followed by the green “live dot” — a nod to the on-air signal — and the Webinars descriptor. Use the official QED logo files when available; this web wordmark is the sub-brand lock-up for digital use.

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the “E” on all sides.
  • Use colour on light backgrounds and white on dark ones.
  • Minimum width: 96px on screen, 25mm in print.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
  • Recolour the wordmark or move the live dot.
  • Place it on busy imagery without a dark overlay.

04 · Typography

One family: Poppins

05 · Layout & space

Room to breathe

A 4px base unit, a 1200px container and generous section spacing. White cards sit on the Off-White canvas — never the reverse. Sections flow into each other with a soft wave divider.

Spacing scale

--space-xs 4
--space-sm 8
--space-md 16
--space-lg 24
--space-xl 32
--space-2xl 48
--space-3xl 64

Grid & breakpoints

  • Container 1200px, fluid gutter 16–32px
  • Long-form text max 800px
  • Cards: 3 columns → 2 at 960px → 1 at 600px
  • Navigation collapses to a menu at 900px
  • Radius: 4px buttons & inputs, 8px cards, 16px feature panels
  • Touch targets ≥ 44px on every interactive element

07 · Voice & tone

Confident, clear, human

We write like an experienced producer briefing a senior client: short sentences, concrete benefits, no jargon. British English spelling (organise, programme).

08 · Accessibility

Readable by everyone

All text must meet WCAG 2.1 AA. QED Green is bright: use it for fills and large elements, pair it with Navy text on buttons, and switch to #127A57 for small green text on light backgrounds. Try any combination below.

— AA text — AA large — AAA —
Webinars that stand out Body copy preview at regular size.

Do

  • Navy text on QED Green buttons.
  • White text on Dark Teal and Navy.
  • Provide captions on all recorded video.
  • Keep a visible green focus ring on interactive elements.

Don't

  • White text on QED Green — it fails contrast.
  • Small QED Green text on Off-White.
  • Pure black text, or font weights below 400.
  • More than two accent colours on one page.
