01 · Essence

Plan it, do it, log it

SportHQ is a training hub for amateurs who mix a club sport with running and the gym. It is part of the HQ family: every HQ shares one style and changes only its accent.

Today first

The app opens on what to do today. The plan, the log and the stats come after.

Numbers you read at a glance

Distances, times and load are set big in a condensed sports font, with the unit small beside them.

Colour means something

Purple is the brand. Green, amber and red only mean done, warning and error. Each sport has its own colour.

HQ family

Hub = ink (black and white), Sport = deep purple, Work = blue, Personal = raspberry. No HQ uses green, amber or red as its accent.

02 · Logo

The stadium q

Lowercase s + hq. The q is a stadium: a running track with one lane line, a football pitch in the infield. The letters are drawn shapes, not a font, so they look the same everywhere. All files come from brand/export.mjs.

Do and don't

  • ✓Use the purple mark on white or the cool grey, the white mark on purple, charcoal or photos.
  • ✕Don't put the purple mark on a dark background: it disappears (1.3 : 1).
  • ✕Don't retype the letters in a font, stretch the mark or add effects.
  • ✕Don't recolour it with a sport colour or with green, amber or red.

04 · Type

The phone's own font, a sports font for numbers

Text uses the system font (San Francisco on iPhone, Segoe UI on Windows): fast, familiar, nothing to load. Numbers use Barlow Condensed, stored in the app, so they work offline and nothing is sent to Google.

07 · Voice

Like a training partner

Plain words, short sentences, the athlete's own terms. The app talks about sessions, runs and matches, not records and entries.

Buttons say what happens

Start workout, Log it, Add a race, Add your result. Not "Submit" or "OK".

Warnings say why

"Football Sat + long run Sun: 2 hard days in a row", not "Rule violation detected".

Sports words, written out

km, /km, m D+, RPE 1–10, PB, taper. Short forms only where space is tight (Run, Gym, Swim on the phone's sport tiles).

Readable in the sun and in the dark

09 · Tokens

For developers

The source of truth is sporthq3/css/style.css. Change a colour there once and it changes everywhere. Logos: edit brand/export.mjs, then run node brand/export.mjs.

  --bg: #e9edf2;          --surface: #ffffff;
  --text: #161922;        --muted: #5b6472;       --border: #d6dce4;
  --accent: #3d0079;      --accent-text: #ffffff;
  --topbar-bg: #3d0079;   --hero-bg: #3d0079;     --strip-bg: #13111a;
  --ok: #15803d;          --error: #b91c1c;
  --warn-bg: #fef3c7;     --warn-text: #92400e;
  --race-mode-accent: #c4a5ff;  --race-mode-button: #5b2a9e;
  --radius: 14px;
  --font: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
  --font-num: "Barlow Condensed", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;
}
