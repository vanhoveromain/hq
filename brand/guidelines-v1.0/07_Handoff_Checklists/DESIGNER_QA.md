# Designer sign-off checklist

- [ ] Parent logo redrawn as clean editable vector from approved uploaded PNG, user signs off at 100% and 16px.
- [ ] Export parent in 1-colour black on white and reverse white on charcoal, document exceptions.
- [ ] Product marks optically balanced and identifiable without colour.
- [ ] Favicon and PWA app icons tested in actual browsers.
- [ ] Primary colours remain exactly the approved HEX codes.
- [ ] Contrast checked for all actual UI states incl. dark mode, focus and disabled.
- [ ] Geist fonts downloaded legally; licensing retained in project.
- [ ] Top nav and HQ switcher tested mobile/desktop including accessibility.
- [ ] Formal typography, spacing, radius and motion tokens signed off.
- [ ] Confirm tagline (currently a proposal).
- [ ] Confirm product access and future-space statuses before publishing.
- [ ] Review logo originality / trademark conflicts before commercial use.
